Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cistr ("the App") handles information when you use the Cistr chess review and analysis application.

1. Information We Collect

Cistr does not operate its own user accounts, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or cloud-storage services. We do not collect personal information through developer-operated servers.

The App does not ask you to create an account or provide a password. Information you enter and analysis you create are handled locally on your device, except where an online feature described below requires a request to a third-party service.

2. Chess.com Public Game Retrieval

If you choose to retrieve public games, you can enter a public Chess.com username. The username is sent to Chess.com's public API so the App can retrieve publicly available game data.

  • The App does not ask for your Chess.com password or login credentials.
  • Only publicly available game information is requested.
  • Retrieved game data is used by the App for chess review and analysis.
  • The App does not send this information to a Cistr-operated server.

When you make a request to Chess.com, Chess.com may receive technical information associated with that network request, such as your IP address, and may process it under its own privacy practices. For more information, review the Chess.com Privacy Policy.

3. Local Storage

The App may store chess games, analysis, and application settings locally on your device to provide its features. This locally stored information is not automatically uploaded to Cistr servers.

If you clear the App's data or uninstall it, locally stored information may be deleted. The availability of device backups is controlled by your operating system and device settings.

4. Internet and Device Permissions

Cistr is designed to support offline use. An internet connection is needed for features that retrieve public Chess.com game data or access other explicitly requested online resources. The App uses device resources and permissions only as needed to provide its functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

The third-party service currently described in this policy is Chess.com, which is used when you choose to retrieve public games. Any information handled by Chess.com is subject to Chess.com's own terms and privacy policy. Cistr does not control the privacy practices of third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Cistr does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not provide user accounts or collect personal information through developer-operated servers, it is not designed to build profiles of its users.

7. Data Security

Cistr is designed to process app data locally on your device. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and protecting access to information stored on it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App's features or data practices change. Any revised version will be published at this page with an updated effective date.
